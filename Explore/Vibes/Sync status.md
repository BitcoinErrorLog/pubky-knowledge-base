# Sync status

**Author:** [@aintnostressin](https://github.com/aintnostressin)
**Added to Vibes:** 8 September 2026

[Sync status](https://sync-status-vibe.aintnostressin.com/home) adds a progress ring showing whether Nexus has indexed a user's writes, including a **Sync delayed** state.

Sources: [Vibes board](https://vibes.pubky.app), [App fork](https://github.com/aintnostressin/pubky-app/tree/feat/sync-status-indicator), [Nexus fork](https://github.com/aintnostressin/pubky-nexus/tree/feat/user-cursor-endpoint).
