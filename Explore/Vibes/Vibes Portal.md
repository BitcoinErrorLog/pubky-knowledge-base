# Vibes Portal

[Vibes](https://vibes.pubky.app) is a public portfolio of self-hosted experiments on the Pubky stack. The board exposes each project's description, host, source repository, stack target, capabilities, and whether it needs a modified stack. A listing is evidence that an experiment is publicly discoverable; it is not a promise that the project is production-grade.

As of 25 September 2026 the board lists eleven vibes: [[Pubky Arena]], [[Chessky]], [[Englishify]], [[Eventky]], [[Graph Explorer]], [[Light mode]], [[Pubky Marketplace]], [[Mention pills]], [[Your Pubchi]], [[Pubky World]], and [[Sync status]].

Pubky App briefly gained an in-app Vibes alert and sidebar entry in [PR #2487](https://github.com/pubky/pubky-app/pull/2487), but that change was reverted by [PR #2524](https://github.com/pubky/pubky-app/pull/2524) before App 1.9.0. The portal itself remains public.

Source: [vibes.pubky.app](https://vibes.pubky.app).
