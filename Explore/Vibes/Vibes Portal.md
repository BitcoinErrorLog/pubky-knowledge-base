# Vibes Portal

[Vibes](https://vibes.pubky.app) is the live public board for self-hosted experiments on the Pubky stack. The board lists eleven vibes: [[Pubky Arena]], [[Chessky]], [[Englishify]], [[Eventky]], [[Graph Explorer]], [[Light mode]], [[Pubky Marketplace]], [[Mention pills]], [[Your Pubchi]], [[Pubky World]], and [[Sync status]]. Eight were added during September 2026.

Pubky App's in-app Vibes discovery entry point shipped in version 1.9.0 through [PR #2487](https://github.com/pubky/pubky-app/pull/2487) and was reverted on 14 September by [PR #2524](https://github.com/pubky/pubky-app/pull/2524). It is not in the app today.

No entry in the public registry sets `hosted.sso`, so shared sign-in hand-off is not switched on for any listed vibe.

Source: [vibes.pubky.app](https://vibes.pubky.app).
