# Chessky

**Author:** [@gcomte](https://github.com/gcomte)
**Added to Vibes:** 6 September 2026

[Chessky](https://chessky-ten.vercel.app) is asynchronous multiplayer chess with players' Homeservers as the only backend. It uses no indexer or application server.

Sources: [Vibes board](https://vibes.pubky.app), [repository](https://github.com/gcomte/chessky).
