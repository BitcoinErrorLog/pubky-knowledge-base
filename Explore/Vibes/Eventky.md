# Eventky

**Author:** [@gillohner](https://github.com/gillohner)

Eventky represents events and calendars as ordinary Pubky posts with three custom kinds and uses a generic-kind Nexus fork.

Sources: [Vibes board](https://vibes.pubky.app), [App repository](https://github.com/gillohner/pubky-app), [Nexus fork](https://github.com/gillohner/pubky-nexus/tree/feat/custom-post-kinds).
