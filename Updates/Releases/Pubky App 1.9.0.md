# Pubky App 1.9.0

**Date:** 16 September 2026
**Status:** shipped

Added full-text content search; people and collections sections in search; onboarding steps for tags of interest and following best matches; custom feed icons and reworked feed tabs; inline article images; a social-graph status badge on profiles; and the `starter_pack` user stream.

The in-app Pubky Vibes discovery entry point shipped in 1.9.0 and was reverted on 14 September by [PR #2524](https://github.com/pubky/pubky-app/pull/2524), so it is not in the app today.

Source: [Pubky App 1.9.0 release](https://github.com/pubky/pubky-app/releases/tag/v1.9.0).
