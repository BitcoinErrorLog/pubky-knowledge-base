# Biography facts

- **1564:** William Shakespeare was baptized at Holy Trinity Church, Stratford-upon-Avon, on 26 April; his birth date is traditionally observed on 23 April. [Folger](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-life/) (reference only).
- **1590s–1610s:** His surviving dramatic and poetic works were written and published or performed across this period; individual dates are often approximate. [Folger](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-life/) (reference only).
- **1616-04-23:** Shakespeare died in Stratford-upon-Avon. [Folger](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-life/) (reference only).

Modern biographical prose is not part of the voice corpus.
