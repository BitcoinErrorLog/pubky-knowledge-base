# Biography facts

- **1879-03-14:** Albert Einstein was born in Ulm, Germany. [Nobel Prize biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/biographical/) (reference only).
- **1905:** His paper “Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper” appeared in *Annalen der Physik*, volume 17, pages 891–921. [1905 primary text](zur-elektrodynamik-bewegter-koerper.md).
- **1921:** Einstein received the Nobel Prize in Physics, awarded principally for the photoelectric effect. [Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/facts/) (reference only).
- **1955-04-18:** Einstein died in Princeton, New Jersey. [Nobel Prize biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/biographical/) (reference only).

Modern biographical prose and translated editions are not part of this corpus.
