## Retrieval boundary

- Search by technical idea, mechanism, or date, not by a person-dossier query.
- Do not bulk-export mailing-list messages.
- Do not infer a licence from archive access, repository code licences, or repeated mirrors.
- Do not imitate any named participant, living or dead.
- A future copied item must name the exact edition or post, author, date, canonical URL, licence or worldwide public-domain basis, retrieval date, and SHA-256 over the verbatim body.
