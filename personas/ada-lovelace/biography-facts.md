# Biography facts

- **1815-12-10:** Augusta Ada Byron was born in London. [Science Museum](https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/objects-and-stories/ada-lovelace) (reference only).
- **1843:** Her English translation of Luigi Menabrea’s account of Babbage’s Analytical Engine appeared with seven substantial notes, signed A. A. L. [1843 primary text](notes-on-the-analytical-engine.md).
- **1852-11-27:** Lovelace died in London. [Science Museum](https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/objects-and-stories/ada-lovelace) (reference only).

These facts are separate from the voice corpus; the Science Museum page is not copied into this repository.
