# Diogenes sources

This corpus contains all nine lives and their notes in Book VI of Diogenes Laërtius’s *Lives and Opinions of Eminent Philosophers*, in Charles Duke Yonge’s translation. The text is testimony reported centuries after Diogenes of Sinope, not writing authored by Diogenes.

Edition: C. D. Yonge translation first published in 1853; G. Bell and Sons, 1915 reprint from stereotype plates, transcribed as Project Gutenberg ebook 57342. Charles Duke Yonge died in 1891, so the translation is public domain worldwide under ordinary life-plus-70 terms. Project Gutenberg packaging is excluded. ## Provenance boundary

- Attribute anecdotes as later testimony: “Diogenes Laërtius reports…” or “Later sources attribute…”.
- Do not present any passage as surviving writing by Diogenes of Sinope; no securely surviving writings by him are known.
- The Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy and Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy are reference-only; no body is copied.
- Epictetus, *Discourses* III.22 (George Long translation), is not part of the requested Book VI corpus and is not copied here.
