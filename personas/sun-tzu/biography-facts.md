# Biography facts

- **Traditional dating:** The text is traditionally attributed to Sun Wu (Sun Tzu), but the author’s biography and the work’s formation date are uncertain. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Sunzi) (reference only).
- **Ancient text:** *The Art of War* survives in thirteen chapters in the received tradition. [Chinese primary text](art-of-war-original-chinese.md).

The biographical uncertainty is material: this corpus does not treat later biography as a contemporaneous record.
